# MR-0003ADR-0002REQ-0001GOV-0001 — Deterministic BAE source continuity and occurrence validation

## Intent

Ensure that manually registered BAE records, historical origin, current authority, source history and repository-wide reference occurrences remain complete, distinct, resolvable, synchronized and deterministic.

## Governance obligation

- The BAE continuity validator must publish stable rule identifiers.
- The BAE continuity validator must resolve every historical origin.
- The BAE continuity validator must resolve every current authoritative source.
- The BAE continuity validator must validate every source-history entry.
- The BAE continuity validator must confirm that the first source-history entry matches the historical origin.
- The BAE continuity validator must confirm that the terminal source-history entry matches the current authoritative source.
- The BAE continuity validator must reject discontinuous source-history transitions.
- The BAE continuity validator must reject circular source-history transitions.
- The BAE continuity validator must resolve governed review evidence for every authority transition.
- The BAE continuity validator must reject an active BAE whose authoritative source is not active and has no explicit lifecycle outcome.
- The BAE origin validator must scan governed Markdown bodies through registered document profiles.
- The BAE origin validator must require one ordinary canonical introductory citation in the declared historical-origin document.
- The BAE origin validator must reject introductory evidence owned by a different historical-origin document.
- The BAE occurrence projector must derive occurrences only from profile-declared BAE reference positions.
- The BAE occurrence projector must include an eligible introductory citation as an ordinary occurrence.
- The BAE occurrence projector must reject unknown BAE identifiers and divergent canonical titles.
- The BAE occurrence projector must preserve provenance as a separate semantic relation.
- The BAE occurrence projector must order occurrences by BAE identifier, document model, document identifier, body path, profile position, line and column.
- The BAE occurrence materializer must be registered in the repository projection materialization registry.
- The BAE occurrence materializer write mode must replace only reference_occurrences.
- The BAE occurrence materializer check mode must remain read-only.
- The BAE occurrence materializer must refuse to create an unregistered BAE.
- The BAE occurrence materializer must emit an empty list for a registered BAE with no occurrences.
- The BAE occurrence materializer must produce byte-equivalent content on repeated unchanged writes.
- The BAE registry checker must compare the stored occurrence fields with an independently derived projection.
- Validation and projection derivation must remain side-effect-free.
- The verification suite must contain positive and negative coverage for derivation, materialization and idempotence.
- The implementation trace must continue to link the existing checker and verification suite to this Governance Requirement.

## Verification obligations

- The verification must cover one BAE whose historical origin is also its current authoritative source.
- The verification must cover one valid authority-transfer chain.
- The verification must cover one valid BAE supersession outcome.
- The verification must cover one valid BAE deprecation outcome.
- The verification must cover a missing introductory citation in the origin document.
- The verification must cover an introductory citation in the wrong document.
- The verification must cover a natural introductory citation that is also projected as an occurrence.
- The verification must cover an unresolved authoritative source.
- The verification must cover a discontinuous source-history chain.
- The verification must cover a circular source-history chain.
- The verification must cover missing governed review evidence.
- The verification must cover an active BAE with a non-active authoritative source and no lifecycle outcome.
- The verification must cover canonical references in every registered BAE reference position.
- The verification must cover ordinary prose outside registered reference positions.
- The verification must cover stale managed occurrence replacement.
- The verification must cover rejection of an occurrence for an unregistered BAE.
- The verification must compare repeated occurrence materializations for byte equivalence.
- The verification must confirm that materialization preserves every non-managed registry field.
- The verification must confirm that validation and derivation leave canonical sources unchanged.

## Failure conditions

- The verification must fail when a historical origin does not resolve.
- The verification must fail when a current authoritative source does not resolve.
- The verification must fail when source history does not begin with the historical origin.
- The verification must fail when source history does not end with the current authoritative source.
- The verification must fail when a source-history transition is discontinuous.
- The verification must fail when a source-history path is circular.
- The verification must fail when authority-transition review evidence does not resolve.
- The verification must fail when an active BAE has no applicable authoritative source.
- The verification must fail when a governed-document origin lacks an eligible canonical introductory citation.
- The verification must fail when introductory evidence belongs to another document.
- The verification must fail when a derived occurrence is taken from an undeclared profile position.
- The verification must fail when an occurrence references an unregistered BAE.
- The verification must fail when a canonical occurrence title differs from the registered BAE title.
- The verification must fail when provenance and reference occurrences are conflated.
- The verification must fail when stored and derived occurrence projections differ.
- The verification must fail when identical inputs produce different occurrence ordering or materialized bytes.
- The verification must fail when materialization changes a field other than reference_occurrences.
- The verification must fail when validation or derivation modifies a canonical source.
- The verification must fail when implementation traceability is missing.
