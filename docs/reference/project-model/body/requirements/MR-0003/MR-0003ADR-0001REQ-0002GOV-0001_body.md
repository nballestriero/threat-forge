# MR-0003ADR-0001REQ-0002GOV-0001 — Valid BAE provenance and dependency direction

## Intent

Ensure that BAE provenance is resolvable and follows the governed document dependency direction.

## Governance obligation

- The BAE provenance validator must resolve every governed document source.
- The BAE provenance validator must resolve every reviewed analytical addition.
- The BAE provenance validator must resolve review evidence for every analytical addition.
- The BAE provenance validator must distinguish origin relations from supporting relations.
- The BAE provenance validator must reject descendant-only origins.
- The BAE provenance validator must reject circular origin dependencies.
- The BAE provenance validator must reject textual occurrence as the sole origin evidence.
- The BAE provenance validator must preserve canonical source identities.

## Verification obligations

- The verification must resolve every provenance source against governed records or reviewed analytical additions.
- The verification must evaluate document ancestry for every document-origin relation.
- The verification must detect circular origin dependency paths.
- The verification must compare repeated provenance resolution results for deterministic equivalence.
- The verification must cover same-document, ancestor and independent-source valid fixtures.
- The verification must cover descendant-only, unknown-source and circular-origin negative fixtures.

## Failure conditions

- The verification must fail when a provenance source does not resolve.
- The verification must fail when an analytical addition lacks review evidence.
- The verification must fail when a document depends on a BAE introduced only by its descendant.
- The verification must fail when an origin dependency path is circular.
- The verification must fail when first textual occurrence is the only origin evidence.
