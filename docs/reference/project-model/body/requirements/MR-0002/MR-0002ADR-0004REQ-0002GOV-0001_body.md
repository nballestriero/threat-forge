# MR-0002ADR-0004REQ-0002GOV-0001 — Coerenza deterministica tra title del registro e H1 del body

## Intent

Keep the structured registry as the canonical title source while preserving one readable and verifiable Markdown representation.

## Governance obligation

- Every governed body must contain exactly one H1 in the form `# <id> — <title>` derived from its registry record.
- Governed records must use `title` rather than the legacy `name` field for the human-readable document title.
- The body H1 must not use a non-canonical separator or an independently authored title.

## Verification obligations

- The verification must compare every governed body H1 with the canonical registry `id` and `title`.
- The verification must detect missing bodies, missing or duplicate H1 headings, legacy separators and title divergence.
- The verification must produce deterministic counts and stable diagnostics through the active complete-model checkers.

## Failure conditions

- The verification must fail when a governed body is missing or its H1 differs from the canonical registry identity and title.
- The verification must fail when a governed record uses `name` as a title field or a legacy header separator is accepted.
